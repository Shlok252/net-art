Sold for parts

A man from two towns over comes with a trailer. He doesn't ask about the photo in the glovebox or the peppermints in the cupholder. He asks about the engine, the mileage, whether the title is clean. You watch them take it apart piece by piece before it even leaves the driveway.

In the rush of the sale, the photograph gets left in the glovebox, and the glovebox gets sold with everything else. You didn't think to ask for it back until it was already gone.

Close the garage door and start again.
